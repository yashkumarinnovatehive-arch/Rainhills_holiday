Welcome to Rivana Villa

Where Luxury Meets the Monsoon Wilderness

“Hidden in the green landscapes of Amboli, Rivana Villa is a peaceful 3BHK private retreat designed for families, friends, and monsoon lovers who want comfort, privacy, and nature together.”

Amboli is famous for its misty, rain-soaked landscapes, cascading waterfalls, and rich biodiversity. Rivana Villa offers a front-row seat to this natural spectacle. Crafted as a royal-natural farmstay retreat, our villa combines the warmth of rustic wood and stone aesthetics with modern hospitality amenities.

Whether you want to witness the pouring rain from our wide balconies, take a walk on the lush private lawns, or gather in the spacious living area for laughter and stories, Rivana is your exclusive sanctuary. Here, the boundaries between the plush indoors and the untamed wilderness dissolve into a serene green haze.

Monsoon Story

Sensory Experiences at Rivana

Slow down, connect with nature, and feel the soul of Amboli's wild rainy season.

Chapter 01

Wake up to misty mornings

Open your eyes to a canvas of pure emerald. Watch the soft fog drift slowly past your window, wrapping the forest trees in a silent, magical white blanket. The air is crisp, cool, and loaded with the fresh scent of mountain rain.

Chapter 02

Enjoy tea with forest views

Cradle a steaming cup of spiced Chai on the wood deck balcony. Hear the rhythmic drops of rain drumming on the canopy as you stare out into miles of untouched, deep green rainforest wilderness. It's just you, your thoughts, and the mountains.

Chapter 03

Spend peaceful evenings in warm villa lights

As dusk falls over Amboli, the villa transforms into a cozy wooden lighthouse in the wild. Warm, amber lighting glows through the windows, casting a golden hue over the private lawns and providing a secure, luxury shelter against the dark, rainy night.

Chapter 04

Explore Amboli’s rainy beauty

Amboli is a paradise for rain-seekers. Walk along wet pathways, witness raw, roaring waterfalls cascading down black basalt cliffs, and walk through mossy paths. Then, return to the absolute privacy and comfort of your premium farmstay stay.
